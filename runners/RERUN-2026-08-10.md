## Byte counts move by 1 to 2, and that is correct

| figure | original | re-run |
|---|---|---|
| classical Certificate | 929 | 930 |
| classical CertificateVerify | 79 | 78 or 79 |
| pqleaf Certificate | 2163 | 2164 |
| pq Certificate / CertVerify | 8100 / 2428 | 8100 / 2428 |

The chains were reminted, so the keys are new. ECDSA signatures vary by a byte
or two because DER integer encoding adds a leading zero when the high bit of r
or s is set. ML-DSA is fixed-length and does not move. **Quote ML-DSA sizes
exactly and classical sizes as approximate**, or a reader reproducing this will
think something is wrong.
